# NIRA

### *When they can't call, NIRA responds.*

**NIRA (Context-Aware Autonomous Emergency Response)** is an intelligent elderly safety system designed to detect emergencies even when an individual is unable to manually request help.

Unlike conventional emergency systems that depend on an SOS button or voice command, NIRA continuously interprets relevant signals and contextual information to identify situations such as falls, prolonged inactivity, abnormal behavior, or other potential emergencies.

When a potential emergency is detected, NIRA assesses the situation, determines the appropriate level of response, and automatically coordinates alerts to caregivers and, when necessary, nearby healthcare or emergency services.

### Core Idea

**Detect → Understand → Assess → Respond**

NIRA aims to bridge the gap between **an emergency occurring and someone being able to ask for help**, providing an intelligent safety layer for independent elderly living.

### Key Capabilities

- 🧠 **Context-aware risk detection**
- 🧍 **Fall and abnormal-activity detection**
- 🔇 **Silent emergency detection without requiring an SOS**
- ⏱️ **Prolonged inactivity and no-response monitoring**
- 🚨 **Automated emergency alert escalation**
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Caregiver notification**
- 🏥 **Healthcare and emergency-service escalation**
- 📍 **Context-based emergency information**
- 🤖 **Autonomous response orchestration**

### Vision

NIRA is built around a simple principle:

> **When a person cannot communicate that something is wrong, the system should be capable of recognizing the signs and initiating the right response.**

The goal is not simply to detect falls, but to create an intelligent, context-aware emergency response layer that helps protect vulnerable individuals when human intervention may be delayed or impossible.
